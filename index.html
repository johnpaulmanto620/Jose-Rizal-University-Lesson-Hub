About This Website

Jose Rizal University Programming Hub is an independent educational resource designed to help first-year students learn the basics of computer programming.

The lessons are general introductory materials and do not represent the official Jose Rizal University syllabus. Your instructor's course outline should remain your main guide.

Built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No database or account is required.
